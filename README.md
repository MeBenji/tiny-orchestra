# Tiny Orchestra
The toy orchestra isn't playing! Play as a toy conductor to direct them and save the song!

## Game Jam
[Tiny Orchestra](https://patriciamendes.itch.io/tiny-orchestra) is our [entry](https://itch.io/jam/theveryseriousjuniperdevgamejam/rate/4719237) for [The Very Serious Juniper Dev Game Jam](https://itch.io/jam/theveryseriousjuniperdevgamejam).

## Where to play
Play it here: [Tiny Orchestra](https://patriciamendes.itch.io/tiny-orchestra)

## How to play
Play as conductor. You're at center of a gramophone, to direct the orchestra that's marching  towards you from the edge. Spin the disc to face each toy, direct them and save the show!

## Controls
+ Spin the disc:
  - A / D
  - Left / Right arrow
  - or Joystick
+ Conduct: Space

## Accessibility
Blind-accessible. Best played if downloaded.
